# Intent: a running verify Job must never be reaped as finished (#1599)

## Problem

On TFactory 0.12.2 the periodic reconciler marked spec `l1-pr1-wc-lab-go-20261119b` `failed` / `verify_job_reaped` ("verify Job finished (deadline/backoff) with no verdict (#464)") at 04:28:17Z. The pod log shows the verify Job was created at 04:28:04Z, 13 s earlier; it was still Running and finished normally about 10 minutes later. The verdict was lost although the Job did the work.

Cause (read from `agents/verify_dispatch.py::_probe_job`): `active` is `bool(job.status.active)`. A Job whose pod is not yet counted has `status.active` unset, so the probe answers "exists, not active, not succeeded". `reap_if_orphaned` reads that as "deadline/backoff exhausted" and reaps. The liveness sweep uses the same probe.

The same campaign also showed `control plane restarted mid-generating (#774)` on three specs although the pod never restarted. The pod log shows the only startup reconcile at 03:49:45Z (no specs stranded) and nothing in code calls `reconcile_inline_orphans` again, so that marker could not be traced to a live bug from the logs that survive; the spec dirs show it next to a vanished `.worktree`.

## Proposed outcome

A Job that exists is reported finished only on positive evidence (a Complete or Failed condition with `status == "True"`, or `succeeded > 0`, the existing meaning). Anything else that exists counts as active, so concurrent and freshly scheduled Jobs are never reaped. A real finished-without-verdict Job is still reaped.

## Affected users and systems

`verify_dispatch._probe_job` and its callers `reconcile_and_reap_once` and `liveness_sweep.job_active_probe`; every kubejob verify run.

## Constraints

- Smallest change; the 404 (Job gone) and API-error (fail alive) branches stay as they are.
- No cluster change.
- Out of scope: the dispatch writes the queued row before creating the Job, so a reconcile tick landing between the two sees 404 and reaps. That window is separate (Codex review finding) and is noted on the issue, not changed here.

## Open questions

The #774 false restart marker is not reproduced. Decision (owner delegation 2026-11-19): fix the proven reaper race now, leave a comment on the issue stating what was and was not found, and keep the issue open for the marker.
